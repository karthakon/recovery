## §3 — THE TWO QUESTIONS, FIXED BEFORE SEARCHING

**REGISTERED IN ADVANCE, BECAUSE A SEARCH WITHOUT A FIXED QUESTION RETURNS SUPPORT FOR WHATEVER IS
ALREADY BELIEVED.**

**Q1. IS RESPIRATION MEASURABLY MORE IRREGULAR IN REM THAN IN NREM IN HEALTHY ADULTS?** By what
measure, and against what reference signal?

**Q2. HAS RESPIRATORY VARIABILITY, DERIVED FROM BEAT INTERVALS ALONE RATHER THAN FROM AN AIRFLOW OR
EFFORT CHANNEL, BEEN USED TO CLASSIFY SLEEP STAGES?** With what accuracy, on what hardware, against
what reference?

**Q2 IS THE ONE THAT DECIDES.** Q1 being true says nothing about whether a wrist PPG that delivers
one interval per beat can see it. **REGISTERED IN ADVANCE: A YES ON Q1 WITH A NO ON Q2 IS NOT
ENCOURAGEMENT. IT IS THE ANSWER THAT LEAVES THE HOLD IN PLACE.**

**SOURCE CLASSES, FIXED BEFORE SEARCHING.** PRIMARY: peer-reviewed studies, meta-analyses and
systematic reviews. **CORROBORATING ONLY: manufacturer material, preprints, patents, encyclopaedia
entries and blogs. NO FIGURE IN THIS FILE RESTS ON ONE OF THOSE.**

**SEARCHED 2026-09-17 by web search over published literature.** Terms: respiratory variability REM
versus NREM breath-to-breath; sleep staging photoplethysmography derived respiration REM accuracy;
wearable sleep staging systematic review; respiratory rate estimation from pulse interval wrist PPG;
cardiorespiratory features REM classification.

## §4 — Q1: YES, AND IT IS WELL ESTABLISHED. MEASURED FROM AIRFLOW, NOT FROM A WRIST.

- **Rostig et al., *Sleep* 2005** (PubMed 16171285) — 29 healthy subjects, age about 26, full-night
  polysomnography with a **pneumotachograph on a full-face mask**. The study's premise, stated as
  already established, is that breathing variability is **higher in REM than in NREM**; its own
  contribution is that the variability is **not random** — long-range correlations are essentially
  absent in NREM timing and drive, and present in REM.
- **Kantelhardt et al., *Physica A* 2002/2003** — detrended fluctuation analysis of breath-to-breath
  intervals and breath volumes. **Long-range correlations appear in REM and wake and are absent in
  deep and light sleep.** The authors note the parallel with heart-rate correlation behaviour.
- **Respiratory-rate-variability study, sleeping adults without obstructive sleep apnea** (George
  Washington University repository copy) — RRV computed from the **expiratory component of the nasal
  pressure signal**, reported **greatest in wake and in REM**, and explicitly agreeing with earlier
  pneumotachograph work.
- **Nguyen et al.** — inter-breath intervals from **nasal cannula pressure**, variability quantified
  by standard deviation, coefficient of variation and a detrended-fluctuation scaling exponent.
  **This is the measurement vocabulary the field uses: SD, CV and a scaling exponent over inter-
  breath intervals.**

**THE LIMIT THAT MATTERS AND IS NOT BURIED: EVERY Q1 SOURCE MEASURES BREATHING DIRECTLY** — a
pneumotachograph, a face mask, a nasal cannula or nasal pressure. **NONE DERIVES ITS SIGNAL FROM
BEAT INTERVALS, AND NONE USED A WRIST-WORN DEVICE.**

**A SECOND LIMIT, REGISTERED BECAUSE IT CUTS AGAINST THE DIRECTION:** more than one source places
**wake above REM** on the same variability measures. **A variability-based rule therefore separates
REM from NREM but does NOT separate REM from wake** — which is the same hazard
`stillness-run-readout-spec-v1` §7 already registers for quiet wakefulness.

## §5 — Q2: NO STUDY WAS FOUND THAT DOES WHAT THIS PROJECT WOULD DO.

**WHAT THE PUBLISHED CARDIORESPIRATORY STAGING WORK ACTUALLY USES:**

- **Redmond and Heneghan, 2006** (PubMed 16532775) — RR intervals, an **inductance-plethysmography
  estimate of rib-cage effort**, and an **ECG-derived respiration** signal. Three states — wake, REM,
  NREM. **Subject-specific classifier: 79 pct accuracy, Cohen's kappa 0.56. Subject-INDEPENDENT:
  accuracy drops to 67 pct.** An EEG-based comparison on the same data reached 87 pct and kappa 0.75.
- **Fonseca et al., 2015 and 2017** — ECG plus **respiratory inductance plethysmography**, 142
  cardiac, respiratory and cardiorespiratory-interaction features. A conditional-random-field
  classifier reached **kappa 0.53 and 70.8 pct on 100 healthy participants**, and **0.45 and 69.7
  pct** on a second group.
- **CReSS, *Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine* 2021** — heart-rate variability plus **airflow**, 296
  polysomnographs, four classes. **78 pct accuracy, kappa 0.643.**
- **PPG plus a respiratory sensor with a CNN-LSTM, 2024** — **three-stage kappa 0.714**, four-stage
  kappa 0.550. **The respiratory input is a separate sensor and the improvement is attributed to
  adding it.**
- **Imtiaz, *Sensors* 2021, systematic review of 90 papers on wearable sleep staging** — EEG is the
  only modality able to identify all sleep stages; **PPG-based systems are easier to wear but are
  unable to identify all the stages.**

**WHAT WAS SEARCHED FOR AND NOT FOUND — AN ABSENCE, RECORDED AS ONE:**

**NO STUDY WAS LOCATED THAT DERIVES BREATH-TO-BREATH RESPIRATORY VARIABILITY FROM BEAT INTERVALS
ALONE, ON A WRIST, AND USES IT TO DETECT REM.** **AN EMPTY SEARCH IS NOT PROOF OF ABSENCE** (RULE
1) — it is a survey over the terms in §3 and over the references of what those terms returned.
**IT IS RECORDED AS "NOT FOUND", NEVER AS "DOES NOT EXIST".**

**WHAT IS ESTABLISHED ABOUT BEAT-INTERVAL-DERIVED RESPIRATION, WHICH IS A WEAKER CLAIM THAN Q2:**

- **Average respiratory RATE is obtainable.** A wrist-worn optical system study concludes that
  beat-to-beat intervals during sleep permit a reliable breathing-rate estimate, **and states the
  reason: the subject is at rest and nearly motionless, so it does not generalise to daily life.**
  It also records that wrist intervals are **an approximation** of ECG intervals.
- **An HRV-high-frequency approach reports mean absolute error under 1 breath per minute** — **but
  only after rejecting windows failing a signal-quality gate, and the paper reports the retention
  rate as the cost.**
- **THE FINDING THAT BEARS MOST DIRECTLY ON THIS PROJECT:** a wearable study in *npj Digital
  Medicine* built on exactly the RSA feature Recovery would use, **and states plainly that its
  algorithm produces one estimate per night and that accurate estimates of quantities such as
  respiratory-rate VARIABILITY are therefore not available from it.** **RECOVERY WOULD NEED THE
  VARIABILITY, NOT THE NIGHTLY AVERAGE.**
- **Wrist respiratory-rate accuracy, where measured:** a consumer smartwatch validated against
  polysomnography in 195 participants reported **root-mean-square error of about 1.1 breaths per
  minute for the overnight average and about 1.6 for continuous measurement** — **and that device
  combines PPG with accelerometer micro-motion, which is not what an interval-only route has.**
  Wrist-worn PPG respiratory activity extracted from **pulse AMPLITUDE** — a waveform feature
  Recovery cannot see — reached an estimation error of about 0.5 plus or minus 1.9 breaths per
  minute only after quality and motion post-processing.

**CORROBORATING ONLY, AND NAMED AS SUCH BECAUSE IT IS A PREPRINT AND NOT PEER-REVIEWED:** a 2025
medRxiv review states that respiratory patterns are the most informative feature for REM detection
while **extracting respiration from wrist PPG remains unreliable**, and that REM and light sleep both
show elevated heart-rate variability, **making them hard to separate by cardiac signals alone.**
**NO CONCLUSION IN THIS FILE RESTS ON IT**; it is recorded because it states the project's own
position from the outside.

## §6 — WHAT THIS PROJECT'S OWN INSTRUMENT ADDS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

**`hrv-cadence-readout-spec-v1` §6.1's PRIMARY NYQUIST TEST IS THE RELEVANT MEASUREMENT AND IT HAS
CLEARED ON EVERY NIGHT IT HAS RUN.** The unique-sample-rate margin against the respiratory band has

BAND IS NOT ALIASED AWAY. IT SAYS NOTHING ABOUT WHETHER THE MODULATION IS STRONG ENOUGH TO MEASURE,
AND NOTHING ABOUT VARIABILITY.**

**`rsa-feasibility-readout-spec-v1` §6's CEILING STANDS AND IS REINFORCED BY §5** — wrist-derived
respiration is reported around 0.5 to 1.5 breaths per minute of error **with fit as the largest
error source**, and the literature this project cites used dedicated channels.

## §7 — WHAT THIS RESULT DOES TO THE HOLD

**THE HOLD ON `classifier-spec-v6` IS NOT LIFTED.**

**Q1 IS ANSWERED YES AND THAT WAS THE CHEAPER HALF.** The REM-irregularity claim is real,
replicated, and measured with airflow instruments in healthy adults.

**Q2 IS ANSWERED "NOT FOUND", WHICH IS NEITHER A YES NOR A PROVEN IMPOSSIBLE.**
`rsa-feasibility-readout-spec-v1` §7 requires "proven impossible" to be a MEASUREMENT AND NOT AN
ARGUMENT, and **a literature gap is not a measurement.** **THE HOLD THEREFORE STAYS, AND IT STAYS
FOR THE REASON IT WAS PLACED: THE SECOND GATE — DOES RESPIRATORY VARIABILITY DERIVED THIS WAY
DISCRIMINATE SLEEP STAGES — HAS STILL NEVER BEEN TESTED BY ANYTHING IN THIS PROJECT.**

**WHAT CHANGES: THE BURDEN, AND IT IS NOW EXPLICIT.** Every located study reaching a usable REM
kappa had **a dedicated respiratory channel, a full PPG waveform, or a subject-specific training
set** — and the best subject-INDEPENDENT cardiorespiratory figure located, Redmond and Heneghan's 67
pct, **is the honest comparison for a device with no training set and one subject.** **A LATER SPEC
PROPOSING RRV MUST STATE WHICH OF THOSE THREE IT IS SUBSTITUTING FOR AND WITH WHAT.**

**WHAT WOULD REFUTE THE DIRECTION, REGISTERED HERE SO A DISAPPOINTING READING IS NOT RE-READ AS AN
ENCOURAGING ONE:** a measurement on this device showing that the interval series yields no
respiratory estimate stable enough to compute variability over a sleep-stage-sized window. **THAT
WOULD BE THE "PROVEN IMPOSSIBLE" FINDING §7 OF THE FEASIBILITY SPEC ASKS FOR, AND
`classifier-spec-v6` WOULD BE DROPPED RATHER THAN HELD.**

**WHAT WOULD CLEAR IT:** a per-epoch respiratory estimate from the interval series whose variability
separates REM from NREM on this subject, on repeated unconfounded nights, **against the same-night
Garmin REM as a proxy and the self-report as the referent** — and **NOT against a threshold fitted to
those same nights.**

**REGISTERED AGAINST THE OBVIOUS SHORTCUT: §4's SOURCES MAY NOT BE USED TO SET A BAND.** Their
figures come from airflow instruments on other people. **A CONSTANT TAKEN FROM THEM AND APPLIED TO
THIS DEVICE WOULD BE A NUMBER FROM A DIFFERENT MEASUREMENT** — the defect class
`stillness-run-readout-spec-v1` §3 names when it refuses to let an onset-calibrated 5 stand as a REM
derivation.
